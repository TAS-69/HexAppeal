# Hex Appeal · Naira

This optional add-on adds Naira of the Greymanes to the main **F9** character list. It follows Hex Appeal's body selection and requires Hex Appeal 1.1.3 or later.

1. Close Crimson Desert.
2. Import the ZIP through **DMM → Mods → Import**, enable **Hex Appeal – Naira**, and mount.
3. Launch Crimson Desert. **F9 → Settings → Character patches** shows the detected patch and its compatibility status. The patch starts enabled, and Naira appears below the witches.
4. Bring Naira into view and select her in the main list to use **Shape** or **Wardrobe**.
5. Preview gear in **Edit [HA] Wardrobe**. **Apply and save** keeps her outfit, **Cancel** returns to the previous outfit, and **[CD] Vanilla** restores her attire, cloak, gloves and boots.

Naira wears Damiane's body in the base game. With **[01] VENUS** she keeps your separately installed Venus Body for Damiane. With **[02] Vanilla** she gets Damiane's original body from Hex Appeal's Vanilla option, the same way Sylvia does. If "No Underwear - NPCs" gives her its underwear-free version of Damiane's body, she keeps that body and the base underwear controls stay unavailable for her.

The game uses records `1004236`, `412` and `444` for Naira. By default they share one F9 entry and one set of Shape and Wardrobe settings. Untick **Combine instances** beside Naira's name in F9 to choose a record and edit its settings separately. Each record starts from the shared settings.

The patch identifies Naira and her stock equipment. The main Hex Appeal runtime handles shaping, equipment, SDK item options and visibility. Wardrobe uses equipment for the current body. A piece still needs to be fitted for that body to sit correctly.

Uncheck the patch in **F9 → Settings → Character patches** to hide her entry and suspend her controls. Recheck it to restore her entry and saved settings. Restart Crimson Desert after changing the patch or body choice to reload her appearance. To remove the patch from the game entirely, untick it in DMM, mount again and restart.

# Hex Appeal · Nevra Yailar

This optional add-on adds Nevra Yailar to the main **F9** character list. It follows Hex Appeal's body selection and requires Hex Appeal 1.1.3 or later.

1. Close Crimson Desert.
2. Import the ZIP through **DMM → Mods → Import**, enable **Hex Appeal – Nevra Yailar**, and mount.
3. Launch Crimson Desert. **F9 → Settings → Character patches** shows the detected patch and its compatibility status. The patch starts enabled, and Nevra Yailar appears below the witches.
4. Bring Nevra Yailar into view and select her in the main list to use **Shape** or **Wardrobe**.
5. Preview gear in **Edit [HA] Wardrobe**. **Apply and save** keeps her outfit, **Cancel** returns to the previous outfit, and **[CD] Vanilla** restores her top and boots.

Nevra Yailar is an elf with her own version of the game's standard female body. With **[01] VENUS** she uses your separately installed Venus Body for Damiane and keeps her own head, ears and height. With **[02] Vanilla** she keeps her original body and proportions.

The patch identifies Nevra Yailar and her stock equipment. The main Hex Appeal runtime handles shaping, equipment, SDK item options and visibility. Wardrobe uses equipment for the current body. A piece still needs to be fitted for that body to sit correctly. Base underwear controls require an existing base mesh.

Uncheck the patch in **F9 → Settings → Character patches** to hide her entry and suspend her controls. Recheck it to restore her entry and saved settings. Restart Crimson Desert after changing the patch or body choice to reload her appearance. To remove the patch from the game entirely, untick it in DMM, mount again and restart.

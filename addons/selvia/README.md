# Hex Appeal · Selvia

This optional add-on adds Selvia to the main **F9** character list. It follows Hex Appeal's body selection and requires Hex Appeal 1.1.3 or later.

1. Close Crimson Desert.
2. Import the ZIP through **DMM → Mods → Import**, enable **Hex Appeal – Selvia**, and mount.
3. Launch Crimson Desert. **F9 → Settings → Character patches** shows the detected patch and its compatibility status. The patch starts enabled, and Selvia appears below the witches.
4. Bring Selvia into view and select her in the main list to use **Shape** or **Wardrobe**. **[01] VENUS** uses your separately installed Venus Body for Damiane. **[02] Vanilla** uses her original proportions.
5. Preview gear in **Edit [HA] Wardrobe**. **Apply and save** keeps her outfit, **Cancel** returns to the previous outfit, and **[CD] Vanilla** dresses her in the top and boots from her game files.

The game can show Selvia without those clothes, for example when a mod removes her inner layers. **[CD] Vanilla** equips the top and boots listed in her game files. Choose **Follow game** in Wardrobe to keep whatever the game gives her instead.

The patch identifies Selvia and her stock equipment. The main Hex Appeal runtime handles shaping, equipment, SDK item options and visibility. Wardrobe uses equipment for the current body. A piece still needs to be fitted for that body to sit correctly. Base underwear controls require an existing base mesh.

Uncheck the patch in **F9 → Settings → Character patches** to hide her entry and suspend her controls. Recheck it to restore her entry and saved settings. Restart Crimson Desert after changing the patch or body choice to reload her appearance. To remove the patch from the game entirely, untick it in DMM, mount again and restart.

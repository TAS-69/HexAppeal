# Hex Appeal · Rucchia

This optional add-on adds Rucchia to the main **F9** character list. It follows Hex Appeal's body selection and requires Hex Appeal 1.1.3 or later.

1. Close Crimson Desert.
2. Import the ZIP through **DMM → Mods → Import**, enable **Hex Appeal – Rucchia**, and mount.
3. Launch Crimson Desert. **F9 → Settings → Character patches** shows the detected patch and its compatibility status. The patch starts enabled, and Rucchia appears below the witches.
4. Bring Rucchia into view and select her in the main list to use **Shape** or **Wardrobe**. **[01] VENUS** uses your separately installed Venus Body for Damiane. **[02] Vanilla** uses her original proportions.
5. Preview gear in **Edit [HA] Wardrobe**. **Apply and save** keeps her outfit, **Cancel** returns to the previous outfit, and **[CD] Vanilla** restores her robes and boots.

The game also has a character called "Stranger" (`1000212`) who uses Rucchia's appearance. By default she shares Rucchia's F9 entry and her Shape and Wardrobe settings. Untick **Combine instances** beside Rucchia's name in F9 to choose **Rucchia** or **Rucchia (Stranger)** and edit their settings separately. Each starts from the shared settings.

The patch identifies Rucchia and her stock equipment. The main Hex Appeal runtime handles shaping, equipment, SDK item options and visibility. Wardrobe uses equipment for the current body. A piece still needs to be fitted for that body to sit correctly. Base underwear controls require an existing base mesh.

Uncheck the patch in **F9 → Settings → Character patches** to hide her entry and suspend her controls. Recheck it to restore her entry and saved settings. Restart Crimson Desert after changing the patch or body choice to reload her appearance. To remove the patch from the game entirely, untick it in DMM, mount again and restart.
